ABOUT MOVIE

The Fast and The Furious is a globally beloved action film franchise that combines dangerous street racing, large-scale heists, and an unwavering theme of family.

The main characters, Dominic Toretto and Brian O'Conner, face dangerous criminals, forming a unstoppable crew.
